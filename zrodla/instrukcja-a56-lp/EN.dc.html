Turntable feet mounting instructions
4
mounting
steps

In the box

4 feet and 4 M3–M6 screws or 4 wood screws 3–6 mm in the chosen size.

No-screws option: reuse the original screws.

You will need

A Phillips screwdriver or an Allen key that fits the screw, a soft blanket and a spirit level.

Remove the platter and secure the tonearm.

1

Unscrew the body completely

Separate the foot into two parts: the body and the tray with the AVC rubber suspension.

2

Screw the tray to the turntable

Turntable upside down on a blanket, rubber facing the turntable. Drive the screw through the centre of the suspension without squashing the rubber.

3

Screw the body back on

Screw the body onto the tray until it sits firmly. Repeat steps 1–3 for the other feet.

4

Level the turntable

Stand the turntable up and put a spirit level on the platter. Turn the body at the lower corner: up to 8 mm of adjustment.
